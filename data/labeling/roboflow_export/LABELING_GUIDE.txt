Roboflow/CVAT labeling guide
----------------------------
Classes: dent, scratch, crack, corrosion, leak, packaging_damage, terminal_damage, label_damage, luminous_product
1. Upload images/ to Roboflow project
2. Draw bounding boxes per damage region
3. Export YOLOv8 format -> data/labels/yolo/
4. Run: python scripts/run_phase3_train.py
